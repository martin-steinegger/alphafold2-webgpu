Experimental · runs locally

Fold a protein in your browser.

AlphaFold2 model-1 PTM and AlphaFold-Multimer-v3 inference on WebGPU. Inference and predictions stay on this device. In MMseqs2 mode, the query sequence is sent to the selected MSA API; single-sequence, multimer query-only, and custom-A3M modes remain local.

The TypeScript runtime preprocesses inputs and orchestrates inference. All learned AlphaFold operations execute as WGSL compute shaders on your local GPU. MMseqs2 search, custom A3M upload, and no-template Multimer-v3 are supported; multimer MSA pairing, templates, relaxation, and model ensembling remain to be implemented.
